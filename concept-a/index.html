Who is Pillar?

Complete players, on the field and in life

Pillar Sports is a youth development program founded in the greater Toledo area. We believe sport, coached with intention, teaches everything a young athlete needs: discipline, resilience, teamwork, and the confidence to compete at any level.

We provide elite coaching, competitive team experiences and structured development for players at every stage, and we develop character as much as skill.
