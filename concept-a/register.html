Register in three steps

Pick a program, choose a date, and check out on our secure registration portal. It takes about two minutes on your phone.

  1. Find your program

    Start on your sport's page (Hockey, Baseball, Softball) or use the program finder.

  2. Pick a date & register

    Tap a date to open our secure EZFacility checkout. Volleyball registers on pillarvolleyball.com.

  3. You're in

    You'll get a confirmation email with everything you need for day one.
